Onboard Systems

Built for the
Last Mile

Every subsystem is co-designed with medical logistics teams — not adapted from consumer drone technology.

01

Thermal Cargo Vault

Active-controlled compartment maintains temperature anywhere from −20°C to +37°C throughout flight. Validated for blood products, mRNA therapeutics, and live tissue.

02

5G + Mesh Telemetry

Dual-band communication with automatic handoff between cellular 5G, satellite uplink, and mesh networks formed by other AeroHeart aircraft in range.

03

Autonomous Identify & Avoid

360° sensing array with lidar, radar, and stereo vision fused by an onboard AI that detects and maneuvers around obstacles in under 40 milliseconds.

04

Chain-of-Custody Seal

Cryptographically signed cargo receipt generated at pickup and verified at delivery. Every handoff is logged to an immutable audit trail accessible to the care team.

Safety Architecture

Designed to
Never Fail

Every critical system has at minimum triple redundancy. The aircraft is certified to continue safe operations with two simultaneous independent failures.

Power system redundancy3×
Navigation redundancy4×
Communication uplinks3×
Mission success rate (all ops)99.97%
Ground impact risk vs. helicopter0.4%
Ballistic Parachute Recovery
Every aircraft carries a ballistic parachute that deploys automatically on catastrophic failure at altitudes above 30m, reducing terminal velocity to under 4 m/s.
Dynamic Safe-Landing Database
The aircraft continuously evaluates 200+ pre-approved emergency landing zones along every route. A divert can be initiated and completed in under 8 seconds.
Geofencing Hard Limits
Hardware-enforced exclusion zones around airports, hospitals, schools, and event spaces. These zones cannot be overridden via software — they are embedded in firmware.
Operator Manual Override
Any licensed AeroHeart operator can assume remote manual control of any aircraft in the fleet from the operations center within 1.2 seconds of request.
